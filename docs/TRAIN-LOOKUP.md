# Train lookup surface

Visitor mode: Operate. Signed-in staff choose Metro or V/Line, then a route,
and inspect evidence before travelling. This is a local extension of the
existing workspace, not a visual identity replacement.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One route lookup, two honest kinds of evidence; never present missing
regional predictions as an all-clear.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve navy workspace chrome, white content, blue controls and
the existing typography. Use Tailwind for added controls and regional results.

STORY: Choose operator and route, scan destination/next station/predicted time,
then consult official notices for coverage outside this feed.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact operator switch sits above the existing lookup.
Regional route selection and refresh precede a readable list; source freshness
and the disruption-coverage warning stay above results, not buried in a footer.

FORM: Precisely specified local extension; no concept seed or raster comp.
The signature interaction switches evidence without reloading workspace chrome.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

No raster assets or new visual system are introduced. Existing repository
product context is docs/PRODUCT.md; no duplicate root product document is needed.
